# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Only people who use this kitchen, standing directly in front of the drawer with a phone in one hand, arriving by link or QR code. The rest of dantedc.dev has its own audience and is out of scope for this record.

## Product Purpose

`dantedc.dev/h2/opendrawer` ("H2" stands for "How To") is a deadpan but genuinely usable manual for operating one kitchen drawer. Success is someone at the drawer following it and getting a clean open and a clean close without popping the catch off.

## Positioning

It documents one real object in full seriousness, including the 3D-printed catch that exists because the drawer slid open on its own.

## Operating Context

The reader is at the cabinet with a hand free for the drawer and a hand holding the phone. The drawer is the top drawer in a run of three, labelled "Utensils", with two bar handles.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 14 pages router, statically exported (`output: 'export'`). No server.
- "How To" is a series. `/h2/opendrawer` and `/h2/turnonac` (a cover, one step: "Don't.", and a Build complete page) share the same booklet, plus one URL per manual page.
- Music plays once the tutorial starts, never on a cover.
- `/h2` itself has no page.
- Copy, troubleshooting and fun facts come from the owner.

## Brand Commitments

The form is a toy-brick building-instructions booklet, unlike the rest of the site. No LEGO name or logo appears anywhere. Copy states steps in the owner's own words.

## Evidence on Hand

- `drawer_1.jpg`: front view of the three drawers.
- `drawer_2.jpg`: corner view of the catch.
- No photo exists of the drawer open or of its interior; those drawings are inferred.

The mechanism, from the owner: one black 3D-printed piece is attached to the drawer face and one to the side of the cabinet. Pushing the drawer in clicks the drawer piece underneath the cabinet piece. Pulling too hard pops the cabinet piece off its fixture.

## Product Principles

1. It has to work at the drawer: legible at arm's length, one-handed, no reading required beyond a line per step.
2. Never break character. The joke is that the manual takes the drawer seriously.
3. Real details over invented ones. Claims about this drawer come from the owner.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Every drawing has a text description, and the manual works with keyboard, screen readers, and reduced motion.
